1. Select Donation Type

Choose the blood component you would like to contribute today.

Whole Blood Donation

The standard and most common donation. Takes only ~15 minutes and is separated into 3 life-saving components.

Frequency: Every 90 Days
Platelets (Apheresis)

Critical for cancer patients undergoing chemotherapy. Takes ~60-90 minutes via automated apheresis cell separator.

Frequency: Every 15 Days
Double Red Cells

Allows you to donate a concentrated dose of red cells. Especially valuable for O-negative and trauma centers.

Frequency: Every 180 Days
Plasma Donation

Provides vital clotting factors, antibodies, and proteins for severe burn victims and clotting disorders.

Frequency: Every 30 Days
